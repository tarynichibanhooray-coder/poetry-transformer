The morning sun rises over ancient hills,
Casting golden light on emerald fields.
A gentle breeze whispers through the trees,
Carrying songs of birds upon the breeze.

The world awakens with a quiet grace,
As shadows fade from every hidden place.
The dew upon the grass begins to gleam,
Like diamonds scattered in a waking dream.

The mountains stand majestic and so tall,
Their peaks embracing nature's ancient call.
The rivers flow with crystalline delight,
Reflecting all the colors of the light.

Each moment holds a beauty all its own,
In seeds that have within the earth been sown.
The life that grows in every leaf and bloom,
Dispels the darkness, banishes the gloom.

So let us pause and witness nature's art,
And feel the wonder stirring in our heart.
For in this world of endless change and grace,
We find our home, we find our sacred place.
